# **App Name**: BigBul Services

## Core Features:

- Business Name Display: Display the business name 'BigBul' prominently on the webpage.
- Business Address Display: Display the provided business address: 4th Main, Arekere, Bengaluru.
- Business Contact Display: Provide a clear and clickable business contact number: 8294924270.
- Services Listing: List the services provided: Technology Services, Training Course, Trading Support, Placement/Internship.
- Certification Display: Display the text 'Government Certified' prominently.
- Personalized Greeting: Generate customized welcome messages on the landing page for different returning users. LLM will act as a tool to choose from existing curated messages based on user browsing history.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Light olive (#A8BFA5) for a modern, approachable and trustworthy feel.
- Background color: Pale light olive (#F2F3F1), close in hue to the primary color but almost fully desaturated, for a calm, modern background.
- Accent color: Teal (#45A39A), which is an analogous hue, and has enough saturation to stand out without being distracting.
- Font: 'PT Sans', a modern and warm sans-serif, suitable for both headings and body text.
- Clean and simple layout with clear sections for each service and contact information.
